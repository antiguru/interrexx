lang/stream_builtin_errors.rex	A `try:` procedure here cannot catch the 93.914 the orx `command` method raises. Measured: a RAISE from any Rexx method body escapes a trap armed in a called frame, while the same raise armed in the main activation is caught and substr('abc') and 1/0 in that same procedure are both caught.
